NewsCache website archive

An offline copy of the two original websites of NewsCache, the caching news server, taken from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine on 2026-10-21. Both sites are long gone: after the snapshots below the Wayback Machine only has 404s for them.

Start pages

  • TU Wien: NewsCache home page, with the user's manual, the USENIX 1999 paper and the master's thesis (only partly captured; the complete thesis is msthesis.ps.gz, PostScript)

  • linuxhacker.at: older version of the section (2006 to 2008)
