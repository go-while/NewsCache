Article Reinstatement Bug

For people familiar with NNTP: This is also the reason why backfill
causes problems for many news readers.

Articles posted to a newsgroup are numbered sequentially in increasing
order.  The active database (the list of newsgroups that can be
requested using the LIST command) contains the low and high watermark
of each newsgroup.  These watermarks represent the lowest and highest
article number available in that newsgroup.  This mechanism can be
used to deduce the number of available or unread articles in each
newsgroup.

    available_articles=high_watermark-low_watermark+1;
    unread_articles=available_articles-read_articles_between_low_and_high

Thus, the newsreader only needs to keep track of the numbers of the
articles that have been read in each newsgroup.  These are typically
stored in your ~/.newsrc file.

Articles, however, may be deleted.  In this case the above estimation
gets inaccurate.  Thus, many news readers mark deleted articles as
read to get a better estimation of the number of (unread) articles in
each newsgroup.  Unfortunately, articles may be reinstated (see the
NNTP specification and the new NNTP draft specification), which
fortunately happens infrequently. 

However, assume that you read news after article 666 has been
deleted. Now, your news reader marks article 666 as read. When the
article gets reinstated (ie, it has been accidently deleted by the
news server administrator), it is still marked as read and thus the
article will be never offered to you as unread.

How can this be a problem in combination with NewsCache?  Assume a
user reads articles 7 and 42 while in online mode.  Afterwards,
NewsCache is switched to offline mode and articles 8 to 41 can no
longer be requested from the upstream news server and appear as if
they were deleted.  Renumbering the articles on NewsCache is not
possible.

It is likely that the news reader has already requested the overview
database (a list of the article number, the poster, the subject and
other information of the articles available in the current newsgroup)
before requesting articles 7 and 42.  The overview database is indexed
by article numbers.  Thus, you need to set up the remapping before the
user requests the overview database which is impossible since you do
not know which articles the user will read beforehand.

Thus, if you use NewsCache in offline mode in combination with a news
reader that is affected by the "Article Reinstatement Bug", you should
always retrieve all articles from the newsgroup you are interested in
before going offline.
